# Privacy policy

**AutoLocalLLM** · Effective November 11, 2026

AutoLocalLLM is made by Louie Cartoon ("we", "us"). Questions or requests: **louiecartoon.business@gmail.com**.

## The short version

- Your chats, notes, files and voice stay on your PC. We never receive them.
- No ads, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data.
- The app goes online only for the things listed below, and you can turn off the optional ones.

## What stays on your PC

Your chats and chat history, notes, settings, scheduled jobs, the files Computer Use works on, voice input (turned into text on your PC), the model's memory notes, undo records, your product key, and your Gmail app password if you set up agent email (encrypted with Windows' own protection for your account). They're kept in the app's data folder; Settings → About & legal shows where. **Settings → About & legal → Delete all my data** erases it.

## What the app sends, and to whom

1. **Anonymous usage stats** (on by default; turn them off any time in Settings → Anonymous usage stats). While the app is open, it sends our server a random ID made only for this, the app version, whether a product key is active, whether it's one of our own test copies, and plain counts: minutes open, prompts, tokens generated, web searches, model searches, models downloaded, app updates, Computer Use actions, and how many notes and scheduled jobs you have. Never chat text, file names or other content. We don't store IP addresses.
2. **Product key check-in** (only while a key is saved). About once a day, and once more when you remove or replace a key, the app sends our server the key, a random ID for this install, the app name and version, and when the key was first used on this PC. We use it to show how many PCs use each key, to work out when a key expires, and to stop keys being shared. Nothing else is sent.
3. **Buying a key.** Payment happens on a Stripe checkout page. Stripe collects your payment details and email under its own privacy policy; we never see your card number. We receive your name, email and the order details from Stripe, and email your key to you from our Gmail account. While checkout is open, the app sends our server a random code so it can activate your key by itself after payment.
4. **Update checks.** The app reads update information from GitHub.
5. **Downloads and model search.** Models and model searches go directly to Hugging Face and the Ollama model registry. The model engines and voices come from GitHub. These services see your IP address, like any website.
6. **Web search** (on by default; turn it off in Settings → Web search). The words your model searches for are sent to DuckDuckGo, or to Brave Search if you add your own Brave key.
7. **Agent email** (product key, only if you set it up). Emails go out through your own Gmail account, using the app password you gave it.
8. **Programs you choose to run.** Commands you approve, and MCP servers you add, run on your PC and can connect wherever they're built to. What they do with data is up to their makers.

The app's window uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows and covered by Microsoft's privacy statement.

## Where it's kept, and for how long

Our server is in the United States, and the connection to it passes through Tailscale. Usage stats are kept as totals. Check-in records are kept while the key exists; a PC stops counting against a key after 30 days without a check-in. Purchase records (name, email, order) are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require, and so we can resend a lost key.

## Cookies and tracking

The app doesn't use cookies, advertising or tracking tools. Its window keeps only its own settings, on your PC.

## Age

AutoLocalLLM is only for people **18 or older**. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 bought a key, email us: we'll refund it and delete their records.

## Your choices and rights

- Turn off usage stats and web search in Settings.
- Remove your product key in Settings → Product key.
- Erase everything on your PC: Settings → About & legal → **Delete all my data**.
- Email us to get a copy of, correct, or delete the records we hold about you. We delete check-in and usage records on request. Purchase records we're legally required to keep are kept, and used for nothing else.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or in California), you may have more rights, including complaining to your data protection authority. We answer requests within 30 days.

## Security

The app talks to our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Product keys are digitally signed. Your Gmail app password is encrypted on your PC.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. For important changes, the app asks you to read and agree again.
